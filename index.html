What is a triathlon?

Three-discipline race with a swim, bike, and run. Between disciplines, athletes change gear in the transition area:

  • T1 — swim-to-bike (swim stuff off, bike stuff on)
  • T2 — bike-to-run (bike stuff off, run stuff on)

A standard Olympic-distance triathlon is usually 1.5 km swim · 40 km bike · 10 km run. This event has a slightly shorter bike course than is traditional.

Overall place is by total time: swim + T1 + bike + T2 + run. Results will be available here.
